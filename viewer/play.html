Controls

            

              
W A S D
move (arrows too); hold Shift to walk quietly

              
Mouse
aim: your unit turns towards it at its turn rate

              
Left click
fire (hold)

              
R
reload

              
Q E
ability in slot 0 / 1, at the mouse

              
F
pick up (press), plant / defuse (hold)

              
1 2
switch weapon slot

              
G X
drop weapon / drop the charge

              
C
cancel the current channel

              
1-0
buy an item (in the buy phase)

              
Tab
play the next living unit of your team (the bot takes back the one you leave)

              
Space
active mode: wait a tick, hold to keep waiting; turn mode: one tick; real time: start / pause

              
P
let time run on its own, or stop it

              
Wheel
zoom
